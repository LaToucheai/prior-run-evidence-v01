## Finding

The study tests when a recorded earlier software failure can support a current claim. Only the study-local matching-evidence case establishes its specified prior-failure witness. Configuration drift, checker revision drift and evidence-integrity mismatch do not establish the same witness; an unavailable reference remains undetermined.

These are results of a deliberately bounded synthetic experiment. They are not findings about deployed-system performance, the existence of a historical operational incident or legal compliance.
