## Published research report

**[When Does a Prior Failure Count? — Research evidence and reproduction record](RESEARCH-EVIDENCE.md)**

The report describes five frozen synthetic scenarios, 23 unit tests, 15 SHA-256 manifest checks, reproduction histories and the limits of the evidence. Reproducing results is not a claim of independent verification, W3C approval, RI conformance or real-world deployment reliability.
